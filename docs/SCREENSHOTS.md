# Portfolio screenshot checklist

Capture real preview output after QA. No screenshots have been captured in this session.

## Desktop — 1440px

- Homepage main hero, category grid, featured collection and promotional/story content.
- Collection with active native filters and sorted results.
- Product gallery with option selectors, pricing and product details.
- Cart drawer with real items, quantities and subtotal.
- Search suggestions/results.
- Theme Editor configurable hero/collection settings and product metafield/size-guide setup.

## Mobile — 390px plus 320px verification

- Header/navigation drawer and main hero crop.
- Two-column catalogue and filter drawer.
- Product gallery, options and purchase states.
- Cart drawer including close/checkout controls.
- Newsletter and footer.

Record viewport, date, preview theme and test conditions. Hide authentication, tokens and customer data. Confirm image rights. Do not substitute a rendered mockup for the real storefront.
